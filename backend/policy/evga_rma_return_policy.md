# EVGA Hardware RMA & Return Policy Guidelines
**Document Reference:** EVGA-WARR-SOP-2024  
**Applies To:** EVGA Graphics Cards, Motherboards, Power Supplies & RMA Replacements

---

## 1. RMA Return Windows & Types
* **Standard RMA:** Customer ships the defective unit to the EVGA distribution hub. Upon physical inspection and receipt verification, a replacement unit is dispatched within 3 business days.
* **Advanced Cross-Shipment RMA (EAR):** Available for registered Tier 2 and Tier 3 original purchasers. A collateral hold is placed on a credit card, and replacement hardware ships immediately prior to receiving the returned unit.
* **Direct 30-Day Refund:** Applies exclusively to items purchased directly from the EVGA Store. Must be in pristine, unopened condition.

---

## 2. Mandatory Packaging & Shipping Standards
Returned hardware must be packaged in compliance with industrial electrostatic discharge (ESD) and transit protection standards:

1. **Antistatic Protection:** Hardware must be sealed within an approved ESD-safe antistatic protective bag.
2. **Cushioning:** Must have at least two inches (5 cm) of dense protective foam or bubble wrap surrounding all sides. Do not use loose foam peanuts.
3. **PCIe & Connector Protection:** The plastic PCIe slot protector and video port dust caps must be reinstalled if available.
4. **Transit Damage Liability:** If a package arrives damaged due to improper consumer packaging, the RMA claim will be rejected and transferred to the shipping carrier as customer liability.

---

## 3. Physical Inspection & RMA Void Conditions
Upon intake, units undergo mechanical, optical, and electrical testing:
* **Physical Damage:** Any cracked PCB, chipped GPU core, or bent bracket voids standard replacement.
* **Burn Marks:** Electrical burn marks on VRM chokes or melted power connector pins are not covered under limited warranty.
* **Serial Number:** The serial number label affixed to the backplate must remain clean, legible, and unremoved.

---

## 4. High-Value Escalation Escrow (Tier 4 Returns)
For enterprise units and graphics cards valued above $2,500:
* Automated auto-approval is permanently disabled.
* Units undergo mandatory forensic laboratory teardown and microscopic X-ray inspection.
* Replacement or credit release is authorized only upon Senior Quality Assurance sign-off.
