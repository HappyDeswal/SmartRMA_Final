## FAQs

### How long is an NVIDIA product’s warranty?

An NVIDIA product’s warranty period varies by product line and region. Select the appropriate product category above for the full warranty period, coverage details, and terms specific to your hardware.

### How do I file a warranty claim with NVIDIA?

For most NVIDIA products, contact your local distributor or reseller first for warranty and support. If your purchase was made direct from NVIDIA or the distributor can't help, follow the warranty claim instructions on the product-specific warranty page above.

### Which NVIDIA products are covered by warranty?

NVIDIA provides warranty information for NVIDIA SHIELD™, NVIDIA GeForce™ Graphics Cards, NVIDIA DGX Spark™, NVIDIA RTX PRO™ Workstation Graphics Cards, NVIDIA Quadro™ Sync, NVIDIA 3D Vision, NVIDIA Jetson™ Developer Kits and Modules, GeForce GTX™ SLI Bridge, NVLink™ and SLI HB Bridges, and NVIDIA IGX™ Developer Kits.

### Where can I find warranty information for NVIDIA GeForce graphics cards?

GeForce graphics cards purchased through NVIDIA partners are warranted by the partner (Add-In Board partner such as ASUS, MSI, Gigabyte, EVGA, or others). Contact the partner directly for warranty service. NVIDIA Founders Edition cards are covered directly by NVIDIA.

### Are legacy NVIDIA products like 3D Vision and SLI Bridges still under warranty?

Warranty coverage for legacy products is determined by the original warranty period and purchase date. Visit the specific product warranty page for current coverage status, end-of-life information, and any available repair or replacement options.
