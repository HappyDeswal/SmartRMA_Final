# NVIDIA Consumer Hardware Limited Warranty & Return Policy
**Document Reference:** NV-HW-WARR-2024.2  
**Effective Date:** October 2024  
**Applies To:** NVIDIA GeForce RTX Founders Edition Graphics Cards, SHIELD TV, and Direct Consumer Hardware Purchases

---

## 1. 30-Day Direct Return Policy
If you purchased hardware directly from NVIDIA (the "Store"), you may return unopened and unused products within thirty (30) calendar days from the date of delivery for a full refund of the purchase price.

* **Eligibility Requirements:**
  * Product must be in pristine, factory-sealed condition with all original packaging, internal plastic shrouds, and accessories intact.
  * Must include the original packing slip and Proof of Purchase.
  * Products that have been unsealed, powered on, or installed are not eligible for direct 30-day refund and must be processed under the Limited Hardware Warranty.

---

## 2. Standard 3-Year Limited Hardware Warranty
NVIDIA warrants that new NVIDIA-branded hardware ("Product") purchased from an authorized reseller or direct store will be free from defects in materials and workmanship under normal, non-commercial consumer use for a period of **three (3) years** from the original purchase date.

### Covered Hardware Defects:
* Silicon anomalies and internal GPU processor die defects under normal factory operating frequencies.
* GDDR6X video memory integrity failures (e.g., persistent checkerboard artifacts, memory address read/write errors).
* Factory solder joint degradation and cold solder fractures under normal thermal cycling.
* Cooling fan motor failure or bearing seizure occurring during standard desktop usage.

---

## 3. Explicit Warranty Exclusions & Customer-Induced Damage (CID)
This Limited Warranty does **NOT** apply to, and NVIDIA expressly disclaims liability for, any hardware defect, failure, or performance degradation resulting from:

### 3.1 External Electrical Overload & Power Connector Damage
* **Section 4.2(c) - Electrical Overcurrent:** Damage resulting from electrical overload, external power surges, improper or fluctuating voltage transients, short circuits, or uncertified power supplies.
* **12VHPWR / 16-Pin Power Connector Scorching:** Scorched, melted, discolored, or oxidized power connector terminals, pins, or socket housing resulting from improper cable insertion, third-party 90-degree adapter failure, cable bend radius violation, or inadequate pin seating.
* Use of non-certified modular PSU cables or third-party power splitters.

### 3.2 Physical Abuse, Neglect, and Accidental Damage
* Dropped cards, bent PCIe mounting brackets, or cracked PCB substrate layers.
* Severed, gouged, or deeply scratched copper circuit traces on the front or back of the board.
* Broken PCIe retention locks, snapped gold contact fingers, or chipped silicon corners.
* Liquid contact, spills, condensation, corrosion, or foreign substance residue.

### 3.3 Unauthorized Modifications & Tampering
* Removal, alteration, defacing, or illegibility of the factory **Serial Number Barcode Label**.
* Broken, pierced, or removed **"Warranty Void if Removed"** tamper-evident screws on the retention bracket.
* Physical modifications, third-party water block installations, shunted shunt resistors, or unauthorized BIOS flashing beyond factory specifications.

### 3.4 Commercial, Industrial & Enterprise Use
* Deployment of GeForce consumer-grade hardware in commercial datacenter racks, cloud computing farms, or cryptocurrency mining operations operating continuously under out-of-specification thermal environments.

## 4. Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) Protocol
To initiate an RMA claim, the customer must obtain an RMA number from NVIDIA Customer Support prior to shipment.

1. **Intake Verification:** Packages received without an active RMA number will be rejected at the warehouse dock.
2. **Vision & Forensic Inspection:** All returned units undergo automated computer vision inspection (optical alignment, pHash deduplication, and anomaly thermal scoring).
3. **Evaluation Boundaries:**
   * **Approved:** Valid warranty claims are resolved with a factory-certified replacement unit of equal or greater performance.
   * **Rejected (Exclusion):** Products exhibiting Customer Induced Damage (CID), electrical burn marks, or tampered serial numbers will be returned to sender at customer expense with a formal Exclusion Notice.
   * **Escalated:** Cases with ambiguous electrical surge evidence or disputed provenance are routed to an L2 hardware forensics technician.
