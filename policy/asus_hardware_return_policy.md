# ASUS Component & Graphics Card Warranty & CID Policy
**Document Reference:** ASUS-RMA-CID-2024.1  
**Applies To:** ROG, TUF Gaming, and Dual Series Graphics Cards, Motherboards, and Expansion Boards

---

## 1. 14-Day Store Return Guidelines
* Products purchased directly from the ASUS Official Store may be returned within **14 calendar days** of delivery.
* **Restocking Assessment:** Unopened items receive a 100% refund. Opened merchandise returned without functional defect is subject to an industry-standard **15% restocking fee**.
* Products purchased via third-party retail partners (Best Buy, Micro Center, Amazon, Newegg) must follow that retailer's return protocol.

---

## 2. Standard 3-Year Limited Hardware Warranty
ASUS warrants that hardware components will remain operational under normal operating conditions for thirty-six (36) months from the purchase date or manufacture serial date.

### Covered Items:
* Video core GPU defects, silicon instability, or driver TDR crash loops at stock frequencies.
* Memory artifacts, missing texture channels, or VRAM bit flip errors.
* Factory cooler failure (axial fan bearing wobble, pump failure on AIO liquid models).
* Internal power delivery component failure occurring under normal transient loads.

---

## 3. Customer Induced Damage (CID) Rejection Standards
ASUS strictly enforces Customer Induced Damage (CID) criteria. Any unit exhibiting CID is ineligible for standard warranty replacement:

| Defect Category | CID Severity Classification | Warranty Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Severed Copper Traces** | &gt; 3 broken PCB surface or sub-surface copper traces | **Out of Warranty (Non-repairable)** |
| **Severe PCB Gouging** | Tool/screwdriver slip penetrating fiberglass weave | **Out of Warranty (Non-repairable)** |
| **Burnt Power Connectors** | Melted 8-pin or 12VHPWR housing / scorched pins | **Excluded (Electrical Overload)** |
| **Dislodged SMT Components** | Sheared capacitors, resistors, or inductors | **CID - Paid Service or Rejected** |
| **Cracked PCB Substrate** | Hairline fractures along PCIe bus or mounting holes | **Out of Warranty (Structural Failure)** |
| **Liquid / Corrosion** | Oxidation, flux residue, or liquid discoloration | **Void (Environmental Ingress)** |
| **Tampered Barcode Sticker** | Peeled, altered, or missing serial number label | **Warranty Void** |

### CID Section 3.1: Electrical Surge & Connector Burn Exclusion
Damage resulting from external electrical faults, improper PSU pinouts, burnt power input terminals, or lightning surges is classified as external force damage and is **not covered** under manufacturer warranty.

---

## 4. RMA Inspection & Dispute Protocol
* Every return received at an ASUS Service Hub is subjected to high-resolution optical inspection under a microscope.
* If CID is identified during triage, photographic evidence will be recorded in the customer RMA dossier.
* Customers may request a secondary review by a Senior Service Engineer by providing proof of certified surge protector deployment or professional installation certification.
